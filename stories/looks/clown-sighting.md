# clown-sighting — look bible

Photorealistic live-action local television news footage, present-day San Francisco in late October, a broadcast news camera, eye-level framing, crisp natural colour, an ordinary 40mm lens, a clean rectangular full-frame image, real adult actors who resemble no celebrity.

- Camera: broadcast news, eye-level, medium close-ups for the speakers, one aerial drone for the cold open, a high corner security camera for the store footage.
- Light: golden dusk, pink dusk, warm dusk, orange sunset, cool blue dusk, flat fluorescent, soft studio key light, flat grey dusk. Halloween everywhere in the outdoor dressing: jack-o'-lanterns on stoops, orange and purple string lights, fake cobwebs, paper skeletons, marigolds and papel picado in the Mission.
- Tone: played completely straight, sincere news; the joke is the gap between the sincere foreground and the small, out-of-focus clown in the background, never noticed.
- Sound: the clean close sound of a television news broadcast, a voice recorded on a handheld microphone, a light city ambience.

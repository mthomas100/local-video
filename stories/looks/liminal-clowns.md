# Liminal Clowns — the liminal dream (no look test; built from five reference photos, not included)

The five reference photos ARE the look: a warm hotel corridor, a tiled pool corridor, the mustard
backrooms, a teal cloud-painted room, a dark green mall escalator. The family of clowns moves
through them as one quiet, uncanny dream. Palette and light come straight from the images; no look
test was rendered because the user supplied the palette.

## The eight slots (shootable words)

1. Palette: warm beige and deep red (hotel corridor), cool white tile and turquoise (pool corridor),
   flat mustard (backrooms), pale teal and white (cloud room), dark green (mall escalator). One
   signature accent: the child's red balloon.
2. Light: low-key, motivated by the places themselves — warm recessed ceiling lights in the hotel,
   a bright cool light at the pool bend, flat recessed fluorescents in the backrooms, bright square
   ceiling panels in the cloud room, a single green escalator glow in the mall. No harsh sun.
3. Camera behaviour: gentle, unhurried — slow push-ins and steady tracking for the faces, slow wide
   shots for the empty spaces. Never handheld jitter; the stillness is the point.
4. Lens feel: shallow depth of field, the background soft; "ordinary 40mm lens, a clean rectangular
   full-frame image". Wides keep the whole frame in focus.
5. Production design and wardrobe: the five liminal spaces exactly as photographed; a family of three
   real adult clowns (white face paint, red noses, tattered costumes with silver bells), the child
   holding a real red balloon. No extra props, no crowds.
6. Weather and time: interior, timeless; the spaces are lit by their own light, no day or night.
7. Pacing: slow and quiet — one steady shot per beat, medium and close for the faces, wide for the
   empty rooms. The dialogue is sparse, tired, and carries them place to place.
8. Sound and score: a low fluorescent hum, a soft water lap, a distant room tone, an escalator
   clunk; sparse, quiet voices.

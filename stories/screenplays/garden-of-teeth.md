# "The Garden of Teeth" (screenplay by Claude Code for pi, 2026-10-03)

A gothic Halloween fairy tale told as one story.
- **Who and why:** Zorya, a pale young woman in black, must cross the garden to get home to the lighthouse before
  night.
- **The rules:** her mother's three rules, said in the first shot: stay on the path, don't let the pumpkins see you,
  never wake the rose. She breaks them one by one.
- **The garden:** it calls to her in her mother's voice. She steps off the path, every pumpkin lights up and turns to
  her, and the Rose wakes: a giant black rose whose round head opens on a ring of teeth.
- **The flight:** the garden swallows her into the endless rooms beneath it. She follows seawater seeping through the
  walls to the foot of the old sea wall that holds the sea back from the garden.
- **The climax:** with the Rose closing in, she opens the floodgate. The sea drowns the garden, the pumpkins and the
  Rose, and catches her too.
- **The ending:** at dawn she wakes on the sand with home in sight. Beside her hand, a tiny black bud opens on a ring
  of teeth.

Every shot follows from the one before it, and nothing in it is random.

## Cast (one CAST portrait; `[cast=zorya]` on every shot marked)

- `zorya`: a young woman of about twenty-five, slender, a smooth very pale porcelain face with high cheekbones, large
  dark grey eyes, dark red lips, long straight jet-black hair parted in the middle and falling to her waist, a
  high-collared black Victorian gown with long sleeves, a fitted bodice and a full skirt.
  - **She is the same woman in every shot,** the brief's first request, so every shot that shows her face carries
    `[cast=zorya]` and her full description.
  - **Her face is clean:** pale skin, grey eyes, dark red lips, and nothing else on it.
- **She is the only person in the film.** The mother is only a voice from the flowers (shot 4) and a tiny distant
  silhouette with a lantern in the lighthouse door (shot 23).

## The Rose and the places (describe them physically in every shot)

- **The Rose:** a real giant black rose head four metres across on a thick thorned stem, taller than every other
  flower.
  - It is round, a ball of black petals. When it opens, the petals part on a ring of long curved ivory teeth around a
    dark throat.
  - Smaller rose heads as big as a person do the same.
- **The garden:**
  - real giant flowers ten metres tall (black roses, blood-red poppies, dark orange lilies) on thorned stems as thick
    as tree trunks;
  - a narrow path of fallen black petals, lined with real carved pumpkins as tall as Zorya;
  - all of it in a deep hollow below a long grey stone sea wall.
- **The sea wall and the sea:** a towering old grey stone wall with a huge rusted iron floodgate and a giant iron
  wheel. The stormy sea on its far side stands higher than the garden, and its waves burst over the top.
- **The lighthouse:** a tall white stone lighthouse with a black iron lantern room, on a black cliff at the end of the
  sea wall. Its beam sweeps the sky.
- **The rooms under the garden:** the endless gothic backrooms.
  - Identical empty rooms with a low ceiling of flickering amber light panels, and thick pale roots breaking through
    the ceiling.
  - Black damask walls with a faded orange pattern, and damp dark red carpet.
  - In every room, a high-backed red velvet armchair and a small carved pumpkin on a little wooden table.

# "Thorns and Static" (screenplay by Claude Code for pi, 2026-10-03)

A gothic Halloween fairy tale in two worlds. Zorya, a two-hundred-year-old vampire with the face of a girl of
twenty, has always been the monster. Tonight everything wants to eat her. A forest of giant living flowers hunts her
at sunset, with thorned vines, jack-o'-lanterns that wake as she passes, and lightning. The ground swallows her and
drops her into the backrooms, endless gothic rooms where everything repeats: the same chair, the same television
showing nothing, and once, herself. A door lets her out onto a black-sand beach at sunset, where for one moment she
has the thing a vampire should never want: the sun on her face. Then the sea draws back, and a tsunami sweeps away
the flowers, the pumpkins and her. The film ends on the television's static, the "nothing on" she complained about.
Nobody explains any of it. She never says she is a vampire, and nobody says what the garden or the rooms are.

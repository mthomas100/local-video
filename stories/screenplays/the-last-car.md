# The Last Car

On a foggy San Francisco night, Marisol — a cable car grip operator of about fifty who has
just run the last car of the night — is walking home up the steep hill when she finds
Theo, a lost and soaked tourist of about twenty-five, stranded at the bottom because the
last car has gone. She teases him into walking up with her, and at the top the fog parts
over the lit city. Warm and funny; the joke is she drives the hill and still has to walk
it, and the warmth is that she does it with him.

## Shape
9:16 portrait, 720p, fast, 8 shots of 8 s (64 s ≈ 1 minute). Every shot carries the two
recurring characters with [cast] and [still]. The five required speaking-shot kinds plus
close-ups: wide held [hold] (shot 1), over-the-shoulder (3), both-speak exchange (4),
orbiting (5), walk-and-talk (6), and close-ups (2, 7, 8).

## Cast
- `marisol` — Marisol, a woman of about fifty: short grey-streaked hair, a worn navy
  cable-car uniform jacket with a brass badge, a small red scarf. Voice: warm, dry, low,
  unhurried, jokes sideways. Manner: a slow head-tilt when amused, watches the hill.
- `theo` — Theo, a young man of about twenty-five: wet brown hair plastered to his
  forehead, a soaked grey hoodie, a thin silver necklace. Voice: clipped, a little
  frantic, then grateful. Manner: shivers, hands in his pockets.

## Sound
A quiet wet San Francisco night: fog, distant traffic, an occasional far cable car bell.

## Shots
1. **Wide held, foot of the hill, 8 s, Marisol.** On a foggy San Francisco night at the
   foot of a steep street that climbs a full block, twin steel cable-car rails set in the
   asphalt, fog so thick the streetlight halos hang like small moons, pastel Victorian
   houses on the slope. A wide shot from across the street: Marisol, a woman of about
   fifty, short grey-streaked hair, a worn navy cable-car uniform jacket with a brass
   badge, a small red scarf, the only person in the shot, stands about a third of the
   frame's height on the far sidewalk, the empty rails behind her, facing the camera. She
   stays planted for the whole shot, feet together, and the camera holds still. Her eyes
   narrow at the fog, a slow exhale, then she says, dry and low, a wry half-smile
   starting: 'Twenty years I pulled that lever, and tonight I get to climb the hill.'
   The streetlights' warm halos in the fog, blue dusk on the houses. Sound: a quiet wet
   street, a cable car bell far away, then silence.
2. **Medium close-up, foot of the hill, 8 s, Marisol finds Theo.** At the foot of the
   same steep foggy street at night, the cable-car rails glinting under the streetlights.
   A medium close-up: Marisol, a woman of about fifty, short grey-streaked hair, a worn
   navy cable-car uniform jacket with a brass badge, a small red scarf, and Theo, a young
   man of about twenty-five, wet brown hair plastered to his forehead, a soaked grey
   hoodie, a thin silver necklace, stand a few feet apart, the only two people in the
   shot. He shivers, hands in his pockets, eyes wide. Her brows lift, her head tilts, and
   she says, dry and warm, a little amused: 'You look like you lost a fight with the bay.'
   Theo stays silent, mouth closed, teeth chattering. The streetlights' warm halos, fog
   curling around them. Sound: a wet drip, a distant car passing.
3. **Over-the-shoulder, foot of the hill, 8 s, Marisol.** At the foot of the steep foggy
   street at night, the cable-car rails dark under the streetlights. An over-the-shoulder
   shot: Theo's shoulder and wet hoodie in the foreground, silent and mouth closed, and
   beyond him Marisol, a woman of about fifty, short grey-streaked hair, a worn navy
   cable-car uniform jacket with a brass badge, a small red scarf, the only two people in
   the shot, her face clear and about half the frame's height. Her brows are drawn, her
   eyes on him, and she says, matter-of-fact and kind, slowing on the word gone: 'The
   last car's gone. That's the joke — I drive the thing, and I still have to walk it.'
   He listens, mouth closed. Streetlights' warm halos in the fog behind her. Sound: the
   fog, a far bell.
4. **Two-shot exchange, foot of the hill, 8 s, both speak.** At the foot of the steep
   foggy street at night, the cable-car rails leading up into the fog. A two-shot, waist
   up: Marisol, a woman of about fifty, short grey-streaked hair, a worn navy cable-car
   uniform jacket with a brass badge, a small red scarf, and Theo, a young man of about
   twenty-five, wet brown hair plastered to his forehead, a soaked grey hoodie, a thin
   silver necklace, the only two people in the shot, turned toward each other. His brows
   lift, her arms fold, then she says, teasing, warm: 'Where were you headed, before the
   fog ate the schedule?' Then Theo answers, defeated, a small shrug: 'The cable car.
   That's the whole plan.' The streetlights' warm halos, fog thick between them. Sound: a
   drip, a far bell.
5. **Orbiting, foot of the hill, 8 s, Marisol.** At the foot of the steep foggy street at
   night, the cable-car rails glinting. The camera slowly circles Marisol, a woman of
   about fifty, short grey-streaked hair, a worn navy cable-car uniform jacket with a
   brass badge, a small red scarf, her face clear and about half the frame's height, as
   she stands beside Theo, a young man of about twenty-five, wet brown hair, a soaked
   grey hoodie, a thin silver necklace, the only two people in the shot. He is silent,
   mouth closed, hands in his pockets. As the camera circles her, her brows rise, her
   chin lifts, and she says, cajoling and warm, drawing out the word up: 'Up. That's the
   only way out of here — the hill's not that long.' The streetlights' warm halos sweep
   past in the fog. Sound: a slow drip, a far bell.
6. **Walk-and-talk, the climb, 8 s, Marisol.** On the steep foggy street at night, the
   twin cable-car rails climbing under the streetlights, pastel Victorian houses on the
   slope. A walk-and-talk, medium shot, the camera tracking alongside: Marisol, a woman
   of about fifty, short grey-streaked hair, a worn navy cable-car uniform jacket with a
   brass badge, a small red scarf, and Theo, a young man of about twenty-five, wet brown
   hair, a soaked grey hoodie, a thin silver necklace, the only two people in the shot,
   climbing the hill together, she a half-step ahead. As she walks, her face to the
   camera, her eyes on him, she says, brisk and warm: 'Come on. I'll walk you up. I've
   got a thermos, and a diner at the top.' Theo walks beside her, silent, mouth closed, a
   wet drip from his sleeve. The streetlights' warm halos, fog thinning a little ahead.
   Sound: footsteps on wet asphalt, a far bell.
7. **Medium close-up, near the top, 8 s, Marisol.** Near the top of the steep foggy
   street at night, the cable-car rails leveling, the fog breaking. A medium close-up on
   Marisol, a woman of about fifty, short grey-streaked hair, a worn navy cable-car
   uniform jacket with a brass badge, a small red scarf, her face clear, the only person
   in the shot. Her eyes are bright, her head tilts, and she says, dry and warm, a laugh
   catching on the word twenty: 'Twenty years I ran that car, and tonight's the night I
   learn what my passengers see from the top.' Behind her the fog parts, city lights far
   below. The streetlights' warm halos, cool blue night. Sound: a soft laugh, a far bell.
8. **Wide button, the top, 8 s, Marisol gives Theo the scarf.** At the top of the steep
   foggy street at night, the cable-car rails ending at a small crest, the fog parting to
   show the lit city far below, a warm diner window glowing. A wide shot from the crest:
   Marisol, a woman of about fifty, short grey-streaked hair, a worn navy cable-car
   uniform jacket with a brass badge, a small red scarf, and Theo, a young man of about
   twenty-five, wet brown hair, a soaked grey hoodie, a thin silver necklace, the only two
   people in the shot, stand at the top of the hill, the fog and city lights behind them.
   She looks at him, then hands him her red scarf, and says, quiet and warm: 'There. You
   made it up. Now the diner's on me.' He is silent, mouth closed, pulling the scarf
   around his wet neck. The diner's warm window glow, the lit city below in the fog.
   Sound: a diner door, distant traffic, the fog.

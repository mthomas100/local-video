# the-last-car — notes

## Premise
A foggy San Francisco night. Marisol, a cable car grip operator of about fifty, has just
run the last car of the night and is walking home up the steep hill. At the bottom she
finds Theo, a lost and soaked tourist of about twenty-five, stranded because the last car
went. She talks him into walking up with her. Warm and funny.

## Game (one sentence)
A woman who has spent twenty years making the hill climbable has to climb it on foot
tonight, and she drags a lost, soaked stranger up with her — the joke is she drives the
thing and still has to walk; the warmth is they do it together.

- No look test (brief says none); style bible written directly.

## Speaking-shot plan (the required five + close-ups)
1. WIDE HELD [hold] — Marisol alone, ~1/3 frame height, the empty rails behind her.
2. MEDIUM CLOSE-UP — Marisol sees Theo; she speaks to him.
3. OVER-THE-SHOULDER — Theo's shoulder in the foreground, silent, mouth closed; Marisol
   speaks over it.
4. EXCHANGE (both speak in one shot) — Marisol and Theo trade two named lines.
5. ORBITING — the camera slowly circles Marisol as she says a line (or profile; pick orbit).
6. WALK-AND-TALK — Marisol walks up the hill beside Theo, toward the camera, and speaks.
7. MEDIUM CLOSE-UP — Marisol's warm funny line (the turn).
8. BUTTON — the ending image: the two at the top of the hill, fog parting, city lights
   below; silent or a last quiet line.

## Beats (performance notes per speaking shot)
- M1: moment = last car gone, empty rails, cold. Want = get walking. Verb = to grumble,
  dry. Subtext = she's tired but it's her job. Beat = dry → wry. Op word = walk.
- M2: moment = she spots a shivering stranger. Want = check if he's hurt. Verb = to ask.
  Beat = concerned → amused (he's fine, just soaked). Op word = soaked.
- M3: moment = Theo can't believe the car's gone. Want = reassure him. Verb = to reassure,
  dry. Beat = matter-of-fact → kind. Op word = gone.
- M4 exchange: Marisol: moment = he's hopeless. Want = get him moving. Verb = to tease,
  warm. Theo: moment = soaked, lost. Want = understand. Verb = to complain, defeated. The
  two lines answer each other; the turn is hers.
- M5: moment = he hesitates at the first step. Want = persuade. Verb = to cajole, warm.
  Beat = wry → generous. Op word = walk (again, but now inviting).
- M6: moment = they're climbing, he's lagging. Want = keep him going. Verb = to rally.
  Beat = brisk → warm. Op word = hill.
- M7: moment = near the top, he's soaked and laughing. Want = land the joke. Verb = to
  tease, warm. Beat = dry → warm. Op word = twenty.
- M8 button: silent image, or one last quiet line from Marisol; the button is them at the
  top with the city below.

## Character voices
- Marisol: a warm, dry San Francisco voice, low, unhurried; jokes sideways; never explains.
  Manner: hands on the lever habit, a slow head-tilt when amused, watches the hill.
- Theo: younger, clipped, a little frantic, then grateful; a flat tourist accent.
  Manner: shivers, pulls his wet collar, keeps his hands in his pockets.

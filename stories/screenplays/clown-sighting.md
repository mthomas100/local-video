# "Clown Sighting" — Channel Six Nightly News (screenplay by Claude Code for pi, 2026-10-03)

A three-minute local TV news report, played completely straight, in Halloween week. A clown has been seen all over San
Francisco for six days, and because it is Halloween week, nobody can tell if it matters. The anchor and the reporter treat it as serious news; the witnesses describe what they saw with total sincerity.
Behind every one of them, small and out of focus, the clown is quietly doing something absurd, and nobody in the
foreground ever notices. The joke is the gap between the sincere foreground and the background. It is never pointed
at, never looked at, never commented on. The last shot pays it off: the clown is the weatherman. Halloween is everywhere in the
set dressing (jack-o'-lanterns on stoops, orange and purple string lights, fake cobwebs, paper skeletons, marigolds
and papel picado in the Mission), which is exactly why nobody notices a clown.

## How every speaking shot is built (the rig's sync rules, measured: follow them exactly)

- ONE visible speaker, a medium close-up from the chest up, the face large in the upper half of the portrait frame,
  facing the camera or three-quarter, face lit, nothing in front of the mouth.
- Witnesses look just off the lens toward the unseen reporter. A black handheld microphone reaches in from the left
  edge of the frame and is held at the witness's chest, below the chin, never in front of the mouth. Kevin holds his
  own microphone low at his chest.
- The speech is the shot's action, right after the speaker's description, then the background, then the light, then
  "Sound: ...". Keep the head still while speaking: eyes and brows act, the body does not lean, walk, bow or look down.
- The clown in the background: "far behind <speaker>, small and out of focus, about a quarter of <her/his> height",
  doing the one action written for that shot. Nobody in the foreground looks at him.
- No smile or grin while a line is spoken. The comedy is the sincerity: say the deadpan in the delivery.
- The place is described physically in every line (San Francisco places drawn as look-alikes otherwise).

# notes — "The Valley of Funding" (working title)

## Premise
A hushed, reverent BBC-style nature documentary (whispered British narrator) films a San Francisco tech
startup office in the Mission during Halloween week — employees as wildlife: territory marks, the great
snack-kitchen migration, the alpha engineer, the costume contest as mating ritual. Cutaway interviews:
employees dead serious about absurd things. Twist: the narrator's voice belongs to the night-shift janitor,
unseen until the supply closet opens. Button: at 2 a.m. the office clown — background of every shot, noticed
by no one all film — slowly turns his head to the lens.

## The game (repeating, escalating contrast)
Grave wildlife-narration register applied to mundane office behavior, each beat topping the last; interview
lines are absolute corporate sincerity about insane things. Escalation ladder: habitat → migration → alpha
rivalry → mating ritual → apex predator (the VC) → the voice itself turns out to be part of the habitat.

## Shape
120 s / 8 = 15 shots, all 8 s. Vertical 9:16, 720x1280 (HD). Documentary spine, in order:
establishing → arrival ritual → interview → migration → interview → rivalry duel → interview → contest
setup → contest/mating dance → predator → winner → predator satisfied → TWIST (narrator = janitor) →
button (clown, silent).

## Conceits (hold to the end)
- The clown (cast, silent, never noticed by any character — not the host, not the last speaker) is in the
  background of every shot, doing ONE simple readable action. Characters never acknowledge him.
- The janitor is a background clue in shots 2, 6, 9, 13 (one beat-too-long look at camera) — he never
  speaks until the reveal. Nobody else reacts to the narrator's voice.
- The narrator is never named on camera until shot 14.

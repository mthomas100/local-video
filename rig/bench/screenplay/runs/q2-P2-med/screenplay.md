# The Clown on the Embarcadero

**Premise:** A serious San Francisco nightly-news report about a clown sighting. Every witness is sincere and specific about what the clown did — slashed tires, stole a laptop, rode the bus, did someone's taxes — while the clown is quietly doing something absurd in the background of the same shot, and nobody notices. The newsroom spine (anchor intro, reporter stand-ups, police denial, sign-off) keeps it deadpan and cohesive. It ends on a button: the clown is sitting in the anchor chair, delivering the sign-off himself, while the real anchor stands helpless behind him.

**Shape:** 9:16 portrait, ~3 minutes, 22 shots (8 seconds each, a few 6 s).

# Notes — "Clown Sighting" (Halloween SF news report)

## The game (one sentence)
Everyone in San Francisco has an elaborate, grievance-filled clown story and tells it with total seriousness while the
actual clown quietly goes about mundane errands in the background of every shot — and not one single person ever
notices him.

## The brief's concrete requests (checklist — every one must appear)
- [ ] ~3-minute total runtime (≈180 s), TikTok aspect ratio 9:16, HD (1080x1920)
- [ ] News report format: newsroom/anchor + reporter in the field + interviews
- [ ] Reporter interviewing people about a clown sighting
- [ ] Interviewees each say what they saw / what the clown did
- [ ] The clown is always doing something in the background, never the focus
- [ ] Nobody notices the clown while giving their interview — ever, in any shot
- [ ] Set in San Francisco (real places, physically described landmarks, local gripes)
- [ ] Absurd, deadpan tone; "you're watching the nightly news"
- [ ] Lots of dialogue; every line written word for word; nothing left to the model
- [ ] Cohesive narrative with escalation and a button ending
- [ ] Halloween vibe in set dressing of every shot + a few lines (never a pun that explains itself)

## Shape
- Aspect 9:16 (1080x1920), HD. 22 shots, mostly 8 s, a few 5-6 s beats = ~172-180 s.
- Spine (news genre, fixed order):
  1. Anchor intro (studio) — 1-2
  2. Reporter on location, introduces sighting — 3-4
  3. Witnesses (escalating claims) — 5-12
  4. Reporter + "evidence" — 13
  5. Official (police/PPA-type figure) — 14-15
  6. More witnesses, peak absurdity — 16-18
  7. Reporter sign-off — 19
  8. Back to studio: anchor + button — 20-22
- Escalation ladder: seen nearby → did chores → helped people → civic pride → official "no evidence" → clown
  everywhere → button: the clown is the anchor's next segment (weather forecast).
- Recurring background character: THE CLOWN (fixed cast description), silent, mouth shut, one simple background action
  per shot. Behind the speaker in every speaking shot except studio shots (where he appears in the last two).
- [quality] on: anchor intro (1), reporter intro (3), police shot (14), button (22).
- [still] shots: none needed (no instant events); keep motion simple.

## Cast (fixed descriptions)
- ANCHOR DIANE VOSS — 50s woman, silver bob, navy blazer, pearl earrings — composed, deadly serious, slight pity.
- REPORTER MARCO REYES — 30s Latino man, dark curly hair, rumpled tan rain jacket, notepad — eager, takes himself too seriously.
- THE CLOWN — 40s man, white face paint, red foam nose, rainbow wig, oversized polka-dot suspenders over baggy plaid pants, giant brown shoes — silent, mouth always closed.
- Witnesses: one-off, described fully in each shot.

## Beat brainstorm (raw, 27)
1. Anchor open: "Good evening. San Francisco is on alert tonight: one clown, at large." Studio desk, Halloween gourd on corner of desk.
2. Anchor toss: "Our Marco Reyes is on Valencia Street, where the sightings keep coming." 
3. Reporter intro on Valencia: fog, painted Victorian houses, pumpkin lanterns on stoops; behind him clown carries a ladder past.
4. Reporter: "Three nights, eleven sightings. The clown does not rob, does not menace. He... tidies." 
5. Witness fisherwoman at Fisherman's Wharf: "He took my nets and mended them. Good stitch. I was furious — where is my afternoon of cursing?" behind: clown folding nets.
6. Tech bro on Market St with electric scooter: "He reset my posture. In one touch. I've been to four practitioners." clown behind straightening parked scooters.
7. Elderly Chinese-American auntie in Chinatown laundry alley: "He gave my nephew a balloon. A BALLOON. Do you know what latex does to a colon?" clown behind hanging her laundry, pegs in mouth.
8. Cop outside precinct: "No evidence of a clown." clown stands in the row of officers behind him holding two coffees, one offered to the cop.
9. Skate punk near the Twist: "He watched us for an hour and left. Judging. That's what that silence was." clown behind measuring the ramp with a tape.
10. Baker in Bernal: "Four in the morning, my ovens were on, sourdough shaped, my apron folded. I fired him. He was never employed." clown behind polishing the display case.
11. Mom at Halloween party lawn with inflatable ghost: "He refilled our candy bowl. The GOOD chocolate. Who does that? That's a targeted subsidy." clown behind inflating a second ghost.
12. Muni driver at doghouse turnaround: "He waited for the light. Wave'd me through. Nobody wave's me through." clown behind in the crosswalk, wave frozen, holding a crossing flag.
13. Reporter "evidence" piece to camera: grainy photo on phone; behind clown rakes the sidewalk leaves into a straight line.
14. Police chief podium on steps, Halloween press banner: "We have reviewed 400 hours of footage. Not one frame contains a clown." clown behind holds up a "closed for filming" sign then a pumpkin.
15. Mayor's aide / official: "The city asks for calm and for residents to secure their gourds." clown behind re-aligning the podium's mic so it's straight.
16. Night watchman at the Painted Ladies: "He complimented my thermos. Didn't say a word. Just looked at the thermos. I cried in my car." clown behind touching his hat to a pumpkin.
17. Two neighbors on porch arguing: "He's a menace." "He fixed my gate." "He's a MENACE who fixed YOUR gate." clown behind gate, oiling hinges.
18. Tourist at Alcatraz-view pier: "In Seattle a clown terrorized us. Here he's just... civic." clown behind feeding a pigeon exactly one fry.
19. Reporter sign-off: "Back to you, Diane." clown behind hangs a wreath on a fence.
20. Studio: anchor "Thanks, Marco." pulls card: "Our final story tonight, the forecast." 
21. Studio beat: anchor annoyed the teleprompter is fogged; "It's been like this since four." 
22. BUTTON: anchor intro "here's Marco with the seven-day forecast" — camera pulls back: the clown at the weather map, rain-dancing a tiny umbrella; anchor deadpan. 
23. Alt button: anchor "and we're told there is no clown" — clown hands her a pumpkin from behind the desk and leaves; she signs off without looking.
24. Witness: Haight vintage-shop owner: "He tried on a coat. Bought nothing. Left the coat on a hanger, buttoned. Like a GHOST with a cardigan." 
25. Reporter being interviewed BY a podcast mic guy: "I have the story" clown behind edits the reporter's car tires-pressing each tire reverently.
26. Anchor cold open tease: "Is it a prank? A performance piece? The PUC says it is neither." 
27. Ending extra-beat: after button, fog rolls across studio floor, clown shoes step through it, guard takes off his cap, clown takes cap, walks off. (too long — cut)

# CLOWN SIGHTING AT THE FERRY BUILDING

**Premise.** A serious, deadpan San Francisco evening-news broadcast about a "clown sighting," where each sincere witness gives a real, specific complaint — a handprint on a truck, a stolen laptop, a burrito eaten, a pumpkin stolen — while the clown is quietly doing exactly that thing in the background, unseen by everyone in the foreground. The game repeats and escalates: the foreground never notices, the background always one-ups the line, and no one ever explains it. The police deny everything while the clown handstands behind them, the claims get bigger, and the button is the reporter's final sign-off: she delivers her line, then slowly turns her head — and finally sees the clown standing beside the tower of pumpkins, holding up a single slice of pumpkin like an offering.

**Shape.** 9:16 portrait, about 3 minutes, 22 shots (mostly 8 seconds each).

**Cast.**

- The anchor, Marco: a forty-five-year-old Latino news anchor named Marco, with a broad face, salt-and-pepper hair in a neat side part, and a navy-blue suit jacket over a cream shirt.
- The reporter, Dana: a thirty-year-old Black woman reporter named Dana, with short natural hair, round wire-rimmed glasses, and a mustard-yellow raincoat over a cream blouse.
- The clown: a clown in a faded orange-and-purple striped jumpsuit, a white painted face with a single black teardrop under one eye, and a small wilted orange flower pinned to the chest.
- Sergeant Reyes: a fifty-year-old white police officer named Sergeant Reyes, with a grey moustache, a silver badge, and a dark blue uniform cap.

**Shot 1 — Studio close-up, 8 s, the anchor.** Inside the news studio, a blue anchor desk with a small carved jack-o-lantern and a tall glass wall showing the San Francisco skyline at dusk. The forty-five-year-old Latino news anchor Marco, with a broad face, salt-and-pepper hair in a neat side part, and a navy-blue suit jacket over a cream shirt, is in a medium close-up, facing the camera, crisp against the blue desk. His eyes settle, his brows lift once, then he says, in a low, steady news voice, each word landing flat and even, 'Good evening. A clown sighting has the city on edge. Dana is at the Ferry Building.' Behind him the skyline glows orange and nothing moves. Warm studio key light on his face, the desk lamp throwing a faint orange glow on the jack-o-lantern. Sound: a low studio tone, then the soft hum of a newsroom.

Behind her, the clown in a faded orange-and-purple striped jumpsuit, a white painted face with a single black teardrop under one eye, and a small wilted orange flower pinned to the chest stands near a parked car and slowly wipes the hood with a giant orange sponge. Flat overcast dusk light, the Ferry Building arches orange behind her. Sound: the low moan of a ferry horn and distant traffic.

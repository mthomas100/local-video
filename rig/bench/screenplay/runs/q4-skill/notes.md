# q4-skill notes

## The game (one sentence)
Everyone interviewed swears they saw a clown do something — and the clown is literally standing right behind them, doing exactly that, in every shot, and nobody once notices.

## The want / spine
News spine, in order: anchor intro → reporter on scene → witnesses (escalating claims) → official → sign-off → back to studio, ending on the button (the clown is the weatherman / the anchor).

## Checklist from the brief
- [ ] ~3 minutes (180 s ≈ 22 shots)
- [ ] TikTok aspect ratio (9:16 vertical), HD
- [ ] nightly news format, newsroom, anchor
- [ ] reporter interviewing people about the clown sighting
- [ ] everyone says what they saw, the clown did this/did that
- [ ] clown always in background, doing stuff, never the focus, nobody notices
- [ ] San Francisco setting, real specifics
- [ ] lots of dialogue, direct, detailed
- [ ] absurdist humor, good dialogue, cohesive narrative
- [ ] Halloween vibe throughout (set dressing + a few lines, never a self-explaining pun)

## Shape
22 shots, most 8 s, a few 5-6 s for short beats. 9:16 vertical. Recurring cast: ANCHOR (in studio), REPORTER, CLOWN (background, never speaks, never noticed), plus one-shot witnesses.

## Candidate beats (brainstorm, no polish)
1. Anchor intro, studio, Halloween set dressing: "A clown has been spotted across San Francisco tonight." Clown is the weatherman behind her, waving.
2. Reporter at the Embarcadero, wind, jack-o-lanterns: "We're live at Pier 39 where the clown was seen."
3. Witness 1, woman with a dog, Pier: "It was doing my taxes. I watched it file my 1040." Clown behind her holds a pencil to a form.
4. Witness 2, guy on a bike, Market St: "It stole my bike and then it gave it back." Clown behind holds the bike, then hands it.
5. Witness 3, old man in a bathrobe, Nob Hill: "It fixed my porch light. Now my porch light is the moon." Clown behind, on a ladder, holding a light.
6. Witness 4, kid with a pumpkin bucket, Castro: "It gave me candy but the candy was broccoli." Clown behind, offering a bucket of broccoli.
7. Witness 5, bus driver, MUNI: "It drove the 14 and it was on time." Clown behind, wiping the bus window.
8. Witness 6, woman with a taco, Mission: "It paid my rent. I don't know how." Clown behind, holding a stack of cash, then slipping it.
9. Witness 7, man with a foghorn, Fisherman's Wharf: "It sang my aria and my aria was better." Clown behind, conducting.
10. Witness 8, librarian, Main Library: "It shushed me. It was very loud about it." Clown behind, finger to lips, loudly.
11. Official, police spokesperson, station: "We have no evidence of any clown." Clown stands in the row of officers, silent.
12. Official 2, city hall clerk: "The clown is not a public safety concern." Clown behind, holding a clipboard, filing.
13. Reporter crosses to a second witness, turns, clown behind them both, nobody notices.
14. Anchor cutaway: "In other news, the fog is fog." Clown behind as weatherman, pointing at a map.
15. Witness 9, dog groomer, Noe Valley: "It groomed my dog and now my dog is a lion." Clown behind, brushing a dog.
16. Witness 10, baker, sourdough: "It baked my sourdough and the bread apologized." Clown behind, holding a loaf, bowing.
17. Reporter wraps: "That's the latest on the clown, back to you." Clown behind, polishing the camera.
18. Anchor sign-off: "Stay safe out there. Happy Halloween." Clown is the weatherman, now in a pumpkin suit, waving.
19. Witness 11, surfer, Ocean Beach: "It fixed my board and the board fixed me." Clown behind, patching the surfboard.
20. Witness 12, bartender, Mission bar: "It poured my shot and the shot poured me." Clown behind, holding a bottle.
21. Reporter asks a witness to describe the clown. Clown behind, in full clown makeup, waving to camera.
22. Button: Anchor: "Here's Bob with your Halloween forecast." Bob is the clown, in a suit, reading a map, silent.

# The Habitat

## Premise

A two-minute nature documentary, narrated in the hushed, reverent tones of a serious British wildlife presenter, set inside a San Francisco tech startup during Halloween week. Every mundane office behaviour — the migration to the snack kitchen, the alpha engineer's standing desk, the costume-contest mating ritual — is described as if the employees were exotic animals in the wild. The employees themselves are interviewed deadpan, completely serious about their jobs, while wearing Halloween costumes. The film escalates from the snack kitchen to the alpha's territory to the CEO's "apex predator" display, and ends on the twist: the narrator, Poppy Whitmore, is not a naturalist at all — she is Head of Growth in a safari costume, and the entire "documentary" is actually the company's Halloween promo video, a demo reel.

## Shape

Vertical 9:16, HD (1080×1920). 120 seconds. 15 shots of 8 seconds each, for a clean 120 seconds.

Sound: her hushed voice over faint keyboard clicks.

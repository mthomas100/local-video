# Clown Sighting
A deadpan Halloween-night news report from San Francisco: reporter Marco Reyes chases eleven sightings of a clown
while every witness has an elaborate, grievance-grade story about what the clown did to them — and in the background
of every single shot the actual clown quietly performs one mundane, helpful errand that not one person in frame ever
notices. The claims escalate from mended nets to civic pride to a police chief's "no evidence of a clown," until the
studio signs off with the seven-day forecast, delivered by the clown himself, rain-dancing a tiny umbrella, while
nobody, including the anchor, remarks on it.

## Shape
9:16 vertical, 1080x1920 HD. 23 shots: nineteen 8 s shots, two 6 s (shots 15, 20), two 5 s (shots 22, 23) — 174 s
total, ~3 minutes. [quality] on shots 1, 3, 13 and 23. No [still] shots. Vertical framing:
speakers are waist-up medium close-ups or full-length; the clown always sits small in the deep background.

## Cast
- `diane` — studio anchor: woman, 55, silver bob cut at the jaw, navy blazer, pearl earrings, composed and fatally serious
- `marco` — field reporter: man, 33, Latino, dark curly hair, rumpled tan rain jacket over a burgundy shirt, notepad, handheld mic, eager and self-important
- `clown` — the sighting: man, 45, white face paint, red foam nose, rainbow wig, rainbow polka-dot suspenders over baggy plaid pants, giant brown shoes; silent in every shot, mouth always closed

## Sound
Nightly-news polish over foggy Halloween-night San Francisco: studio air-hum and paper rustle, a synthetic news sting;
street ambience with distant Muni screech, bay fog-horns, wind, porch-door creaks, and kids' distant trick-or-treat
shouting.

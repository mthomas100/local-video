# The Clown Sighting (San Francisco Halloween News)

A live nightly-news report about a clown sighting across San Francisco on Halloween night. Every person interviewed is certain they saw the clown do something — do their taxes, steal and return their bike, pay their rent, drive the 14 on time. And in every single shot the clown is standing right behind them, doing exactly that, and nobody once notices. The conceit holds to the end: the clown is never acknowledged, never questioned, never the focus — until the button, where the clown is revealed as the weatherman, silent.

## Shape
9:16 vertical (TikTok), HD 1080x1920. 22 shots: 20 at 8 s, 2 short beats at 5 s. Shots 1 and 22 get [quality]. One speaker per shot; the clown is behind every speaker, silent, mouth closed.

## Cast
- **Val (the anchor)** — studio anchor: 50s, silver bob, navy blazer, cream blouse, desk with a pumpkin.
- **Maya (the reporter)** — field reporter: 30s, dark hair pulled back, teal windbreaker, chest mic.
- **Bob (the clown)** — the sighting: 40s, round, white face paint, red nose, rainbow wig, oversized yellow suit with orange buttons, always in the background, never speaks, always silent with his mouth closed.

## Sound
Halloween-night street ambience: distant foghorns, cable-car bells, occasional jack-o-lantern glow, and a quiet studio hum under the anchor.

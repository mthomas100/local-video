# The Clown Report

**Premise:** A deadly-serious San Francisco evening news segment covers a clown sighting. Every witness, the reporter, and the police are interviewed completely straight while the clown quietly does escalating absurd things in the background that nobody notices. The joke is the gap between the sincere front and the clown's escalating background antics; no one ever looks. It ends on a silent reveal: the clown is alone in the newsroom, holding a jack-o-lantern, turning it to face the camera.

**Shape:** 9:16 portrait, about 3 minutes, 22 continuous shots (most 8 s, a few 5-6 s), rendered one at a time.

**Cast:**
- The Anchor: Ava Torres, a 40-year-old anchor with a sharp brown bob, a navy blazer, and red lipstick, seated at a polished news desk.
- The Reporter: Dana Reyes, a 35-year-old field reporter with a dark bob, a bright yellow raincoat, and a black handheld interview microphone held at her chest.
- The Clown: a lanky clown in a faded red-and-orange striped suit, a white face with a painted red nose, a single green wig curl, and oversized yellow shoes.

# THE CLOWN SIGHTING
### An Evening News Report, San Francisco

**Premise (the joke of the film and how it ends):**
A serious, deadpan San Francisco evening news report about a clown sighting on the Embarcadero. Every witness describes a perfectly ordinary thing the clown did — he walked by, he waved, he gave a balloon — while, completely unnoticed behind them, the clown is quietly doing something absurd and escalating: stacking pumpkins, hanging Halloween lights, doing a handstand, juggling. Nobody notices, because the foreground is so sincere. The report builds to the anchor signing off, then noticing the clown standing on her desk, and the film ends on one silent image: the clown alone in the morning fog, building a giant pyramid of pumpkins on the empty Embarcadero.

**Shape:** 9:16 portrait (TikTok aspect ratio), about 2 minutes 45 seconds, 22 continuous shots (mostly 8 seconds, a few 6 seconds). One speaker per speaking shot, in a medium close-up or close-up, facing camera or three-quarter, microphone held at the chest below the chin. The clown never speaks and is never the focus of any shot.

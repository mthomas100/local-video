# The Clown Behind You

**Premise.** A local San Francisco news reporter, Rosa Delgado, is live on a Halloween night covering a "clown sighting." She interviews one witness after another, and each witness calmly describes the clown doing something absurd — juggling jack-o-lanterns, riding a unicycle, stealing a pumpkin, tap-dancing — while the clown is literally doing that exact thing just behind them, in the background, unseen by anyone. Every witness is oblivious, and Rosa keeps missing him too, until the very end she turns and finds the clown standing right behind her shoulder. The clown calmly says he was the point of the whole show, does a backflip, vanishes in a puff of smoke, and leaves only a single carved pumpkin. Rosa holds up the pumpkin and signs off the broadcast with "Happy Halloween, San Francisco." The joke is that the clown is always there, doing exactly what people describe, and nobody — not the witnesses, not the reporter — ever looks.

**Shape.** 9:16 portrait (TikTok aspect), about 3 minutes, 22 shots: 17 shots at 8 seconds and 5 short shots at 6 seconds.

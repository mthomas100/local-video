# THE NIGHTLY CLOWN

**Format:** Vertical 9:16 (1080x1920), HD — TikTok/nightly-news style.
**Runtime:** ~3:00 (180s)
**Setting:** San Francisco, the week of Halloween. Dense fog, pumpkins everywhere, jack-o'-lanterns, bats, orange string lights, cobwebs.

**Logline:** A field reporter interviews five eyewitnesses about a clown sighting in San Francisco. Each witness describes the clown doing something increasingly dramatic — while the clown, always in the background of the shot, is quietly doing something entirely different, and no one ever notices.

**Running gags (must be present in EVERY shot):**
1. The clown is always in frame, doing something mundane and absurd.
2. Nobody — not the reporter, not the witnesses, not the anchor — ever reacts to the clown being in the shot.
3. A squeaky rubber-horn "HONK HONK" plays in the background of every interview. Nobody reacts.
4. The clown never speaks. It only honks.

**Cast:**
- ROSA DELGADO — field reporter (mid-20s, orange Halloween scarf, mic with a little pumpkin taped to it).
- DANA WHITFIELD — news anchor (late 40s, jack-o'-lantern on her desk, orange-and-black blazer).
- SAL — pumpkin vendor (70s, flannel, straw hat).
- DENISE — Muni bus driver (50s, reflective vest, bus-route cap).
- MARCUS — Halloween costume-shop clerk (30s, skeleton-print shirt).
- GLORIA — church lady / community organizer (60s, floral cardigan, holds a casserole dish).
- VIC — clipboard man (40s, polo shirt, name tag that says "VIC," holds a clipboard).
- THE CLOWN — never speaks, only honks. Red wig, white face, big orange nose, oversized polka-dot suit, yellow gloves, a tiny rubber horn.

## SHOT 1 — COLD OPEN (0:00–0:10, 10s) — EXTERIOR, FOGGY SF STREET AT DUSK

**FRAME:** Vertical 9:16. Wide-ish. Deep fog, warm orange streetlight glow. Pumpkins stacked on a wooden cart at frame right. A jack-o'-lantern with a flickering candle sits at frame left. Bats made of cardboard hang from a fire escape above. Trick-or-treaters (two kids in cheap costumes) pass in the deep background, slow.

**CAMERA:** Static. Slight slow push-in (1m over 10s). Low angle, looking up slightly at ROSA.

**ROSA** stands center-frame, holding a microphone with a tiny pumpkin taped to it. Fog rolls. A bat swings.

**ACTION:** Rosa takes a breath. A faint "HONK HONK" from somewhere off-frame. She doesn't react.

**ROSA (to camera, urgent, breathy):**
"The clown is still out there. In San Francisco. On Halloween week. And tonight — everybody has a story. Nobody has a name."

**SOUND:** Squeaky "HONK HONK." Rosa's eyes flick left for a tenth of a second. She recovers.

**ROSA (continues):**
"We're live."

**CUT.**

## SHOT 2 — NEWSROOM INTRO (0:10–0:22, 12s) — INTERIOR, NEWSROOM

**FRAME:** Vertical 9:16. A news desk, warm tungsten. A jack-o'-lantern sits on the desk next to a coffee mug. Cobwebs drape from the desk lamp. Two cardboard bats hang from the ceiling. Behind DANA, a blurry "SAN FRANCISCO NEWS" wall.

**CAMERA:** Static, medium shot. DANA center-frame behind the desk.

**DANA** reads from a teleprompter, warm, authoritative.

**DANA (smooth):**
"Good evening. I'm Dana Whitfield. It's Halloween week, and tonight's top story is a clown. Multiple eyewitnesses across the city report a clown sighting — a clown doing everything from juggling pumpkins to handing out candy. No one knows where it came from. No one knows where it went. Our Rosa Delgado is out there on the street with the people who saw it. Rosa?"

**ROSA (VO, slightly tinny, off-camera):**
"Thanks, Dana. I'm standing on Market Street — and the first witness is right here."

## SHOT 3 — INTERVIEW 1: SAL, THE PUMPKIN VENDOR (0:22–0:57, 35s) — EXTERIOR, PUMPKIN STAND

**FRAME:** Vertical 9:16. Tight two-shot. SAL is foreground center-lower (60% of frame). A pumpkin stand behind him with a hand-painted sign: "FRESH PUMPKINS. $5. NO REFUNDS."

**BACKGROUND BLOCKING — THE CLOWN:**
In the upper-background of the frame, just behind the pumpkin pile, stands THE CLOWN. He is NOT juggling. He is slowly, carefully stacking small pumpkins into a smiley face shape on the ground. One pumpkin at a time. He steps back, looks at the smiley face, adjusts one pumpkin. He is holding a candy apple. He takes a single bite out of the candy apple, sets it back on the pile, and stands perfectly still, staring at one small pumpkin.

**CAMERA:** Static medium two-shot. SAL center. Clown visible upper-right background the whole time.

**ROSA (holding mic to SAL, earnest):**
"Sir — you were here when the clown showed up. Tell me exactly what you saw."

**SAL (gruff, nodding, not looking at the camera):**
"I seen it. I seen the whole thing. He come up to my pumpkin stand, and he just... he started juggling. Flaming pumpkins. Three of 'em. Big ones. He caught every single one, and then he set 'em down and they was still on fire, and he ate a marshmallow right off one of 'em. I ain't never seen nothin' like it."

**BACKGROUND:** The clown is still standing perfectly still, staring at the small pumpkin. He is not juggling. He is not on fire. He does not have a marshmallow.

**ROSA:**
"Flaming pumpkins. And where is the clown right now?"

**SAL (points, confident, still not looking):**
"Right there. Look at him go. He's still juggling. He's been at it for twenty minutes. Look at his hands. Blur."

**BACKGROUND:** The clown has not moved. He is still staring at the small pumpkin. A "HONK HONK" sounds. Sal doesn't react.

**ROSA (turns to look at the pumpkin pile. Sees the clown. Pauses. Turns back to camera, deadpan):**
"...He's not juggling."

**SAL (waves it off):**
"He's a professional. He juggles with his heart. You can't see the heart."

**CUT.**

## SHOT 4 — INTERVIEW 2: DENISE, THE BUS DRIVER (0:57–1:32, 35s) — EXTERIOR, NEAR A MUNI BUS

**FRAME:** Vertical 9:16. Medium two-shot. DENISE foreground center-lower. Behind her, a red-and-silver Muni bus, doors open. A Halloween decoration — a fake spider on a string — hangs from the bus-stop sign. Fog.

**BACKGROUND BLOCKING — THE CLOWN:**
In the upper-background, across the street, THE CLOWN sits on a wooden bench. He is NOT on a bus roof. He is NOT juggling traffic cones. He is wearing a tiny witch hat (too small for his head). He is reading a newspaper upside down. He occasionally honks a small rubber horn: "HONK HONK." No one reacts. After a moment he puts the newspaper down, takes the tiny witch hat off, and tries on a tiny top hat that is even too small.

**CAMERA:** Static. DENISE center. Clown visible in background behind the bus, upper-right.

**ROSA:**
"Ma'am — you drive the 5 bus. You saw the clown."

**DENISE (emphatic, hands on her hips):**
"Oh, honey, I saw it. It was standing on the roof of my bus. Right there on the roof, juggling traffic cones. Big orange ones. And it was honking the whole time. I had to pull over twice. I had a full bus. The passengers was terrified."

**BACKGROUND:** The clown is on the bench, reading an upside-down newspaper, honking. He is not on the roof. He is not juggling cones.

**ROSA:**
"On the roof. Juggling cones. And it's still... where is it now?"

**DENISE (points, without turning, full confidence):**
"Right there. I'm tellin' you, it's still juggling the cones. You can't miss it. It's right there by the bench."

**ROSA (looks at the bench. Clown is now trying on the tiny top hat, holding it with both hands, staring at it):**
"Ma'am. The clown is sitting on a bench. He's trying on a tiny hat."

**DENISE (not even looking):**
"That's the bench. That's where he juggles the cones. Every day. Rain or shine. You just wait for the cones. They come later. It's a build."

**SOUND:** "HONK HONK." Denise doesn't flinch.

**ROSA (deadpan):**
"A build."

**CUT.**

## SHOT 5 — INTERVIEW 3: MARCUS, THE COSTUME-SHOP CLERK (1:32–2:07, 35s) — EXTERIOR, HALLOWEEN COSTUME SHOP

**FRAME:** Vertical 9:16. Medium two-shot. MARCUS foreground center-lower. Behind him, the costume-shop window: mannequins in skeleton costumes, a witch costume, a vampire costume. A sign: "HALLOWEEN. 50% OFF. NO REFUNDS." Orange string lights across the window.

**BACKGROUND BLOCKING — THE CLOWN:**
Inside the shop window, upper-background, THE CLOWN is NOT dancing with a skeleton. He is, one by one, turning every mannequin to face the wall. Slowly. Deliberately. Then he takes a tiny crown out of his pocket, puts it on his own head, and stands perfectly still, facing the window, smiling. No skeleton. No tango.

**CAMERA:** Static. MARCUS center. Clown visible in the shop window, upper-right.

**ROSA:**
"You work at the costume shop. The clown came in."

**MARCUS (animated, excited, hands wide):**
"It came in, yeah. It walked right past the register and it started dancing with the skeleton. The big one in the window. It grabbed the skeleton and they did a little tango. I got it on my phone. It was beautiful. It was like... two lost souls. And then it bowed to the skeleton. It bowed. And I applauded. I applauded, Rosa."

**BACKGROUND:** The clown is turning mannequins to face the wall. He is not dancing with a skeleton. There is no tango. He is not bowing.

**ROSA:**
"You applauded. And the clown is... still in the window?"

**MARCUS (points at the window, still not looking):**
"It's right there. Look. It's still dancing. It's got the skeleton by the hand. They're twirlin'."

**BACKGROUND:** The clown is now wearing the tiny crown, standing perfectly still, facing the window. Every mannequin faces the wall. No skeleton. No twirlin'.

**ROSA (turns. Looks. Long pause):**
"The skeleton is... the skeleton is not dancing."

**MARCUS (confident, unbothered):**
"He's shy. He don't dance when you're lookin'. That's the thing about a skeleton. They're all show-offs till you point a camera."

**SOUND:** "HONK HONK." Marcus doesn't react.

**ROSA (to camera, deadpan):**
"Noted."

**CUT.**

## SHOT 6 — INTERVIEW 4: GLORIA, THE CHURCH LADY (2:07–2:42, 35s) — EXTERIOR, CHURCH STEPS / COMMUNITY HALLOWEEN PARTY

**FRAME:** Vertical 9:16. Medium two-shot. GLORIA foreground center-lower, holding a casserole dish with foil. Behind her, church steps decorated with a cardboard jack-o'-lantern and a banner: "ST. ANNE'S HALLOWEEN PARTY. CANDY. CASSEROLE. PRAYERS." Fog, warm.

**BACKGROUND BLOCKING — THE CLOWN:**
On the church steps, upper-background, THE CLOWN is NOT handing out candy. He is sitting alone with a giant plastic bag of candy corn. He is eating handfuls of candy corn directly out of the bag. Slowly. He then offers a single piece of candy corn to a pigeon. The pigeon ignores him. He holds the candy corn out, waits, shrugs, eats it himself.

**CAMERA:** Static. GLORIA center. Clown visible on the steps, upper-right.

**ROSA:**
"You were at the church's Halloween party."

**GLORIA (warm, big voice, casserole held like a shield):**
"The church. St. Anne's. We had a party for the kids. And the clown — it come in and it started handin' out candy. And not the cheap candy, honey. The good candy. The full-size chocolate. The kind you hide from your own kids. It had a whole garbage bag of it. A garbage bag. Full. I saw the bag."

**BACKGROUND:** The clown is eating candy corn from the bag and offering a single piece to the pigeon. He is not handing out full-size chocolate. There is no garbage bag of good candy.

**ROSA:**
"A garbage bag of full-size chocolate. And it's still handing out candy?"

**GLORIA (gestures toward the steps, without turning):**
"It's right there. It's still handin' it out. Every kid got a full-size bar. Look. It's got the bag open right now."

**BACKGROUND:** The clown is eating candy corn, holding one piece out to the pigeon. The pigeon has flown away. The clown eats the piece.

**ROSA (turns. Sees it. Pauses):**
"Ma'am. He's eating candy corn. He's offering it to a pigeon."

**GLORIA (beatific, unbothered):**
"That's how he starts. He eats a little. Then he hands out the good stuff. You just wait. It's a process. It's a blessing. He's a blessed clown."

**SOUND:** "HONK HONK." Gloria doesn't react.

**ROSA (to camera, slowly):**
"...A blessed clown."

**CUT.**

## SHOT 7 — INTERVIEW 5: VIC, THE CLIPBOARD MAN (2:42–3:00, 18s) — EXTERIOR, PARKING LOT

**FRAME:** Vertical 9:16. Medium two-shot. VIC foreground center-lower, holding a clipboard, wearing a name tag that says "VIC." Behind him: a parking lot, orange cones, fog. A string of orange lights.

**BACKGROUND BLOCKING — THE CLOWN:**
In the parking lot, upper-background, THE CLOWN is riding a small red tricycle in a slow circle. He is NOT stealing a name tag. He is NOT honking at anyone's face. He rides one circle, stops, picks up a small pumpkin, and places it carefully on the tricycle seat. Then he rides one more circle with the pumpkin on the seat. Then he stops, takes the pumpkin off, and holds it up to the sky, staring at it.

**CAMERA:** Static. VIC center. Clown visible in the lot, upper-right.

**ROSA:**
"Sir, you called the station with a very specific report."

**VIC (precise, clipped, reading from clipboard):**
"Yes. I'm a man of facts. I keep a clipboard. I saw the clown at 4:17 PM. It looked me dead in the eye. And it honked. Twice. Then it stole my name tag. Off my shirt. It put the name tag on its own chest and walked away with it. That's a crime. That's a crime, and I want it documented."

**BACKGROUND:** The clown is riding the tricycle with the pumpkin on the seat. He has no name tag. He is not stealing anything.

**ROSA:**
"It stole your name tag. And it's... still wearing it?"

**VIC (points, no looking):**
"It's right there. It has my name tag. Look. 'VIC.' It's wearin' my name. I can see it from here. It's right there on its chest."

**BACKGROUND:** The clown stops the tricycle, takes the pumpkin off, holds it up to the sky, staring at it. No name tag. No chest.

**ROSA (turns. Sees. Long pause. Then, gently):**
"Sir. The clown is holding a pumpkin. He's looking at the sky."

**VIC (unshakeable):**
"That's a decoy. The name tag is underneath. It's a sophisticated clown. It's a criminal clown. I'm writin' it down."

**SOUND:** "HONK HONK." Vic doesn't react. He writes something on his clipboard.

**CUT.**

## SHOT 8 — NEWSROOM CLOSE (3:00–3:15, 15s) — INTERIOR, NEWSROOM

**FRAME:** Vertical 9:16. Medium shot. DANA behind the desk. Jack-o'-lantern on the desk. Cobwebs. Bats.

**BACKGROUND BLOCKING — THE CLOWN:**
Behind DANA, upper-background, THE CLOWN stands directly behind the anchor desk. He is holding a small pumpkin. He is wearing the "VIC" name tag on his chest. He waves slowly at the camera. DANA does not turn. The clown honks: "HONK HONK." DANA flinches slightly, recovers, keeps reading.

**DANA (wrapping up, smooth):**
"So — the clown remains at large. Multiple witnesses. Multiple claims. Zero arrests. If you see the clown: do not approach. Do not feed it candy corn. And do not let it take your name tag."

**BACKGROUND:** The clown is now holding up the small pumpkin, like a news prop, nodding at the camera. He is not stealing anything. He waves again.

**DANA (continues, unaware):**
"That's the news at eleven. I'm Dana Whitfield. Happy Halloween, San Francisco."

**BACKGROUND:** "HONK HONK." DANA's eyes flick — she blinks, pauses a half-second, keeps going. The clown waves the pumpkin at the camera, then sets it down on the desk behind her, next to the jack-o'-lantern.

**DANA (beat, to the producer off-camera):**
"...And we're clear."

**CUT.**

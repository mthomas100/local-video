# Director looks, as words the model can shoot (never use the name in the prompt)

| you want | write |
|---|---|
| Wes Anderson | static locked-off frame, subject dead centre, symmetrical room, pastel pink and mint palette, flat even light, a slow lateral pan, deadpan delivery |
| Wong Kar-wai | handheld, neon magenta and green from signs, rain on glass, slow shutter smear on passing crowds, a woman alone at a noodle counter, cigarette smoke curling |
| Kubrick | slow steady push-in down a symmetrical corridor, one-point perspective, cold fluorescent light, an unblinking stare into the lens |
| Malick | golden hour backlight, wheat or tall grass, handheld drifting camera at hip height, a hand trailing through the grass, whispered voice-over |
| Fincher | cold desaturated teal, low practical lamps, very steady slow dolly, faces half in shadow, rain outside |
| Tarkovsky | long static shot, water dripping in an abandoned interior, mist, a candle carried across the frame, very slow |
| Spielberg | slow push-in on a face lit from below by something off-screen, awe, a warm glow rising, a child's-eye low angle |
| Nolan | wide 70mm-feel landscapes, a ticking clock, cross-cut between two places, cold blue and steel, IMAX-like stillness |
| Sofia Coppola | soft pastel daylight, a young woman looking out a window, lace curtains moving, dreamy pop-song feel, static frames |
| Denis Villeneuve | monumental scale, one small figure against a huge wall or dune, dust haze, low rumble, slow crane |
| Hitchcock | dolly zoom (push in while the background pulls away), a stair seen from above, a hand on a railing, high contrast |
| Iñárritu | one continuous handheld take following a character through doors and crowds, natural light, breathing rhythm |
| Studio Ghibli (only if animation is wanted) | hand-painted 2D animation, soft watercolour skies, wind in the grass, a quiet meal scene |

Combine at most one look with one location. Looks are camera + light + colour + rhythm; the
story stays yours.

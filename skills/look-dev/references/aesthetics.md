# What makes something feel cinematic, as choices the model can shoot

Eight slots. A look is one pick per slot, written as concrete words. Fill all eight.

1. **Palette**: two dominant colours and one accent (teal shadows and amber skin, one red
   coat · bone white, slate, one gold · pastel mint and pink · monochrome grey with one
   yellow umbrella). Say them in the scene ("teal shadows, warm amber skin").
2. **Light**: high key (flat, soft, few shadows) or low key (one source, deep shadows); the
   source and direction; motivated by something in frame (a window, a lamp, a fire, neon).
3. **Camera behaviour**: locked-off and symmetrical · slow dolly and orbit · handheld and
   breathing · long static takes · tracking alongside. One behaviour per film.
4. **Lens feel (safe words only)**: "shallow depth of field, the background soft" (long-lens
   intimacy) · "deep focus, everything sharp" (wide, documentary) · "close and slightly
   distorted at the edges" (wide-angle closeness). Never name a focal length as a format.
5. **Production design and wardrobe**: three period or place objects; a wardrobe palette per
   character that never changes; textures (wool, wet leather, brushed steel, birch bark).
6. **Weather and time**: dawn mist · flat noon · golden hour backlight · blue hour · sodium
   night · rain · snow · heat haze. Move it forward across the film.
7. **Pacing**: shot length. Contemplative (one move per 15 s) · classical (two shots, a cut
   to a close-up) · brisk (three shots, "cut to" twice). Faces medium or close for feeling.
8. **Sound and score**: ambience bed · one signature sound · score in a genre (slow
   accordion waltz, solo cello, a synth pulse, a brass fanfare) · dialogue style (sparse
   and dry, or warm and quick).

## Presets (each a full bundle; combine with a place and a story)

- **Sunlit Slavic fairy tale**: birch-white and gold, one red garment; golden hour and
  candle; slow orbit and push-in; shallow focus; carved wood, samovar, embroidered linen;
  snow then thaw; classical pacing; wind in birches, a balalaika, sparse dry lines.
- **Neon rain noir**: magenta and cyan on black; low key from signs and headlights;
  handheld, close; shallow focus with rain on glass; wet trench coats, a yellow umbrella;
  night rain; brisk; rain bed, a distant siren, a lone saxophone heard far off (sound goes in SOUND=, never in the BIBLE: the image model draws it), clipped dialogue.
- **Baltic melancholy**: slate, sand, sea-green, one orange jacket; flat overcast; locked
  frames and slow pans; deep focus; concrete, wool knits, a ferry; grey afternoon; long
  takes; wind and gulls, solo cello, few words.
- **Golden-hour Americana**: wheat and denim, sky blue; backlight, dust; tracking alongside
  at hip height; shallow; pickup trucks, diners, screen doors; late summer evening;
  classical; cicadas, a slide guitar, easy warm talk.
- **Soviet-modernist sci-fi**: concrete grey, oxide red, mint; cold fluorescent; slow steady
  dolly down corridors; deep focus; brutalist halls, dials, wool uniforms; eternal indoor
  night; contemplative; a hum, a Geiger click, a choir drone, formal speech.
- **Storybook pastel** (live action): mint, pink, cream; high key; locked-off symmetrical
  frames; deep focus; hand-painted signs, uniforms, suitcases; bright day; brisk deadpan;
  a ticking clock, a harpsichord, quick flat delivery.
- **Candlelit gothic**: black, bone, one crimson; single candle from below; slow push-ins;
  shallow; stone, velvet, iron keys; night, fog; contemplative; dripping water, a music
  box, whispers.
- **Documentary natural**: no palette control, real colours; available light; handheld
  follow; deep focus; real streets; whatever the day gives; classical; location sound,
  no score, overlapping natural talk. (The safest photoreal base on this model.)

## Quick translations of references

- "like a Kodak still from the 70s" → warm faded reds, soft highlights, grain NOT mentioned.
- "like a music video" → brisk pacing, one signature colour, tracking moves, score named.
- "like a fairy tale book" → symmetrical frames, one enchanted object per scene, high key.
- "like a war film" → desaturated, handheld, deep focus, mud and wool, distant thunder.
- "dreamy" → backlight and haze, slow motion words ("very slowly"), a hum, soft focus edges.
- "epic" → one small figure against a huge landscape, a slow crane, a low rumble, brass.
